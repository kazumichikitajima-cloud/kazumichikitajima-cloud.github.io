Privacy Policy

Kyukanbi: Alcohol-Free Days (iOS)　Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how the app "Kyukanbi: Alcohol-Free Days" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not send your information to the developer's servers or to any third party. It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not collect personal information such as your name or email address, and no account or sign-in is required.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your drinking log (dates, whether you drank, and the type and number of drinks)
  • Settings (your alcohol-free days of the week, usual drinks, price per drink, reminder times and so on)

This information is stored only on your iPhone. It is deleted when you delete the App.

3. Apple Health (optional)

Only if you turn it on in Settings, the App writes the number of drinks on days you drank to "Number of Alcoholic Beverages" in Apple Health. One drink is counted as 8 g of alcohol (one unit) in the U.K., 10 g in Japan, and 14 g (one U.S. standard drink) everywhere else. The App does not read any data from Apple Health. You can delete the data it wrote at any time in the Health app.

4. Reminders

Evening and morning reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. No information is sent outside your device for reminders.

5. Export

Exports of your log (JSON or CSV) are created only when you choose to export. You decide where they are saved or shared.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

7. Changes

This policy may change as features are added. Any changes will be posted on this page.
