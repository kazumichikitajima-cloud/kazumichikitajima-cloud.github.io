Kyukanbi: Alcohol-Free Days

One tap each morning. Alcohol-free days and alcohol, counted by the week

Kyukanbi is an app for drinking less, where all you do is log yesterday with one tap each morning. Choose which days of the week are your alcohol-free days, and the count starts fresh every week. There are no streaks to break, so a day that slips is no reason to give up.

No ads. No subscription. Logging itself stays free.
